# Asset sources and attribution

The mascot frames and Schale Scans logo in `assets/sprites/` were extracted from the public [Claude artifact supplied for this project](https://claude.ai/artifact/DGNc9CcHD6tcRVMf89ERXT) on October 2, 2026. The images are original downloaded PNG files; their pixels have not been modified or regenerated. The application renders them with `image-rendering: pixelated` and adds its own separate interaction effects.

The [Schale Scans website](https://www.schalescans.com/) credits its original kemomimi mascot artwork to **naga u**. Preserve that attribution when integrating or redistributing these project assets. The logo belongs to Schale Scans. No new license or ownership claim is made here, and no explicit asset license was established from the supplied artifact. These assets were retrieved at the user's request for this Schale Scans companion implementation.
